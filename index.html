About

PT. Yamatogomu Indonesia is a manufacturer of Rubber Parts for automotive with long experience in material and molding. Also, we promise to meet our customer requirements of Quality, Cost, and Delivery date. Please consider us as a good partner to help you achieve your requirements or target.

Philosophy

Creating the future with “Bonds of Trust” and “Particular Manufacturing,” PT Yamatogomu Indonesia builds strong relationships with customers, employees, suppliers, and the community while continuously refining its technology through human resource development. By establishing a reliable production system, the company delivers products with pride and confidence, creating rubber components that contribute to the development of new lifestyles.

Vision

We create affluent and safe lives through the use of rubber materials. Since its establishment during Japan’s period of rapid economic growth, the company has developed through manufacturing rubber products used in household appliances and automobiles. Although these rubber components are often unseen by consumers, they play essential roles in supporting the functions of various devices that are indispensable to everyday life.
Recognizing the importance of every component, we continuously refine our rubber processing technology to deliver high-quality products at reasonable prices. Through this commitment, we strive to support safer, more comfortable, and better lives for people.

Certification

IATF16949 Implementing IATF 16949 ensures that customers receive consistent, good quality products and services, which in turn may bring many business benefits. IATF 16949 specifies requirements for a Quality Management System when an organization wants to: Demonstrate its ability to consistently provide products that meet customer and applicable statutory, regulatory and product safety requirements Enhance customer satisfaction through the effective application of the system Implement processes for improvement of the system.
